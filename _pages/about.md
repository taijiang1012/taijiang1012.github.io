Welcome to my homepage! 👋

I am a Master's student in the **Department of Industrial and Operations Engineering (IOE)** at the **University of Michigan, Ann Arbor**. 

My primary research interest lies in **Human-AI Collaboration** (Human-AI Teaming). I am passionate about understanding how humans and artificial intelligence can work together efficiently, with a specific focus on trust calibration, bidirectional transparency, and mixed-initiative decision-making. I aim to design human-centric AI systems that augment, rather than replace, human expertise in complex and high-stakes environments, such as **Healthcare Systems Engineering** and operations management.

Prior to joining UMich, I developed a strong interdisciplinary background bridging business, data, and technology. I hold an undergraduate degree with a focus on Auditing Informatization and a rigorous quantitative foundation. My industry footprint includes practical experience as an **AI Agent Operations Intern at ByteDance** and a **Strategy Operations Intern at Meituan**. These roles provided me with first-hand insights into how large-scale AI tools and algorithms interact with human operators in real-world, dynamic business scenarios. 

Currently, I am expanding my technical toolkit through advanced coursework in operations research, dynamic programming, stochastic processes, and generative AI applications. 

I am always open to discussions regarding Human-AI collaboration, healthcare engineering, and innovative AI applications. Feel free to reach out!
